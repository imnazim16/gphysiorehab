Ask us anything ↗
What conditions can physiotherapy treat? +

Physiotherapy can help with pain, injury, stiffness, weakness and recovery after surgery across many areas of the body.

How long does a session take? +

Your first assessment usually takes 45–60 minutes. Follow-up sessions are tailored to your plan.

How many sessions will I need? +

This depends on your goals, symptoms and progress. We review your plan with you as you go.

Do you provide home physiotherapy? +

Yes. Ask our team about home visits and availability in your area.

Is physiotherapy painful? +

Treatment should feel manageable. We explain each step and adjust the approach to your comfort.
